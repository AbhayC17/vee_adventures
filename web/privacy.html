The short version

Movement Missions has no ads, no accounts, no social features, no tracking and no links to other websites. We do not collect personal information about children.

What stays on the device

The child's first name or nickname, age, favourite movement style, stars, discoveries and progress are saved only on the device or browser being used. They are not sent to us. Clearing the browser data or uninstalling the app erases them.

Pictures in SPOT (AI feature)

In the SPOT activity a grown-up or child may choose a photo so Vee can name an object. That one photo is sent securely to our server and on to an AI service (Groq) only to identify the object, and the answer comes straight back. We do not keep the photo, and we do not use it to train anything. A "Pick from pictures" option lets children play SPOT without any photo. “Discover anything” (optional, off by default)

A grown-up can turn on “Discover anything” in the grown-up area. When it is on, a photo taken in SPOT can be of almost any object, and the AI also writes the object's name and one or two short, simple facts. Pictures that show people are refused and nothing about them is written. Before a child sees any AI-written text it is checked by our own word filters and by a second safety check on our server. If the checks cannot run, the facts are left out. The photo is used only for that one request and is not stored. A grown-up can turn this off at any time. Optional story helper (parent area)

A grown-up can ask for a short story. Only the name of a recent discovery (for example "Apple") is sent to create it. No personal details are sent.

Cookies, analytics and ads

None.

Children

This game is designed for children aged 3–6 to play with a grown-up nearby. Settings and the activity report are behind a grown-up check.

Your choices
